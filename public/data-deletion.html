Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: November 19, 2026

To request deletion of your HangOut account and associated personal data, email admin@hangoutbeta.com from the email address associated with your account.

Email a deletion request

Include in your request

  • The email address associated with your HangOut account.
  • Your first name and the sign-in method you used (for example, Facebook), to help us locate the account.
  • If you cannot email us from the account address, briefly explain that so we can arrange another way to verify your request.

Do not send your password, Facebook password, access tokens, or a photo of your ID in the email.

What happens next

HangOut currently has no in-app self-service account deletion. After receiving your email, we will review the request and may ask you to verify that you control the account. We will then process the request and reply to confirm the outcome or explain if some information must be retained. Do not treat signing out or having an account deactivated as a deletion request; deactivation is reversible and does not delete your account.

A verified deletion request covers the HangOut account and associated personal data, including profile details, uploaded profile content, pinned places, and service activity, subject to legal and safety retention requirements. Some records may be retained when required to address safety issues or comply with law. Information in backups may persist for a limited period as described in the Privacy Policy.

Facebook sign-in

Requesting deletion from HangOut is separate from removing HangOut's access in your Facebook settings. If you also want to disconnect the app from Facebook, remove it from the Apps and Websites section of your Facebook account. Removing that connection alone does not delete your HangOut account; send the email request above to ask HangOut to delete it.

Contact

For questions about a deletion request, contact admin@hangoutbeta.com.
