Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how HangOut handles personal information when you use our website and app. HangOut is a closed-beta social discovery service for adults. Please do not use the service if you are under 18.

Privacy contact: admin@hangoutbeta.com

Information we collect

  • Account and sign-in information: email address for email accounts; authentication identifiers and basic profile details returned by Google or Facebook when you choose those sign-in options; and your first name and adult-confirmation status.
  • Profile information: first name, date of birth, photos, bio, dating intention, hangout personality, optional gender identity and discovery preferences, and optional Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok profile links.
  • Places and location lookup: places you choose to pin and any place stories you provide. If you choose current-location lookup, your browser shares coordinates with OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find a nearby place. HangOut stores the place you select, not the raw coordinates from that lookup.
  • App activity and communications: likes and passes, matches, messages, meetup proposals, and information you submit in a report or block.
  • Optional identity-review material: if you request manual ID review, you provide an image of a government-issued photo ID. The image is re-encoded to remove image metadata and is available only to authorized HangOut admins or moderators while review is pending. The image is deleted after a decision or withdrawal; if deletion cannot be completed, it remains in a cleanup-pending state until deletion succeeds.
  • Technical information: authentication session data stored in your browser and information needed by our hosting and infrastructure providers to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, operate profiles and discovery, show people who share places, enable matches and conversations, support meetup planning, respond to safety reports, review optional identity requests, maintain and troubleshoot the service, and meet legal obligations. We do not use exact live location to rank or display nearby members.

What other users can see

People eligible to discover you may see the profile details and places you choose to share, including your first name, age, photos, bio, dating intention, disclosed gender identity, personality, and shared-place stories. Your discovery preferences are private. Optional social profile links are available only to an active mutual match. Messages and meetup details are available to the participants in that match. Authorized admins or moderators may access information needed for account operations, safety reviews, or an ID review.

How information is shared

We use Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage. If you choose Google or Facebook sign-in, that provider processes your sign-in and returns information needed to authenticate your account. OpenStreetMap Nominatim processes place searches and, only when you request current-location lookup, the coordinates needed to find a nearby place. These providers handle information under their own privacy terms. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or others.

Retention and security

We retain account and service information while needed to provide and protect HangOut, resolve safety issues, and meet legal obligations. Some information may remain in backups or safety records for a limited period after it is no longer active. ID images follow the deletion process described above. We use access controls and other safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You can choose what profile details and places to share, whether to use social sign-in, and whether to request ID review. You may sign out at any time. To ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to withdraw a pending ID-review request, contact HangOut using the privacy contact above. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request and may retain information where the law or safety obligations require it.

Children

HangOut is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use the service. If you believe a minor has provided information to HangOut, contact us using the privacy contact above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will update the effective date above when we do. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies to future use, subject to any notice or consent required by law.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: admin@hangoutbeta.com
