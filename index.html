Three steps from your favourite spot to a first date

Unlike Tinder or Bumble, which start with photos, HangOut starts with places.

  1. Sign up

    Use your email or continue with Facebook. Add your first name and a short intro.

  2. Pin your hangouts

    Search for up to five places you visit often, or use your current location to find spots near you.

  3. Match and meet

    See people who share your places. Say hi, then meet at a spot you both already know.
